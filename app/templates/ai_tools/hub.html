Smart Daily Briefing

One-page morning view — overdue, today's tasks, at-risk deals, hot accounts. AI tells you what to focus on first.

Outreach Insights

Read every contact's notes and classify the outcome — how many engaged, who said "wrong POC," who asked to reconnect later, who's out of the org.

Sales Coach

Reads your last 30 days of activity and tells you exactly what to do this week — specific accounts, specific actions.

Strategy Advisor

Which industries and accounts should you double down on? AI looks at your pipeline mix and recommends.

Win/Loss Analysis

AI patterns from your last 180 days of closed deals — what you win on, what you lose on, signals to watch.

Rate Card Recommender

Search past deals by role/skill/geo and get median, average, and range from your own pipeline. No AI required.

LinkedIn / WhatsApp Drafter

Short, conversational outreach for LinkedIn DM and WhatsApp — copy-ready, with placeholders.

Meeting Summarizer

Paste raw meeting notes — get a structured MOM with summary, attendees, action items, and any hiring requirements raised. Auto-creates tasks for action items.

JD / Requirement Analyzer

Paste a job description — get extracted role, seniority, must-have skills, experience years, location, employment type, rate, urgency.

AI Email Composer

Go to Contacts, select recipients, click Send Email, then Generate with AI. Produces personalized templates with placeholders that adapt per recipient at send time.

Deal Next-Step Suggester

Open any deal and click Suggest in the AI panel on the right — AI analyzes deal age, stage, last activity, value and notes, then recommends the next concrete action with urgency.

Objection Handler

Paste an objection (e.g. "your rates are too high") — get a coached response in your tone, plus the tactic behind it and 2 alternative angles.

Pre-meeting Research Brief

Open any company or contact and click AI Brief in the header — generates a one-pager: overview, talking points, discovery questions, watch-outs, expansion opportunities.

AI Follow-Up Drafter

On any logged call, meeting, or email, click AI follow-up — opens a draft email that references what was discussed, ready to edit and send.

Dashboard Narrator

Plain-English paragraph at the top of Reports — explains pipeline health, win rate, biggest signal, top risk, and the one opportunity to act on this week.

Capability Drafter

Enter a prospect's industry — get a tailored capability statement positioning your services for that domain. Grounded in your Company Profile so it never invents services you don't offer.

Proposal Generator

Open any deal and click Generate Proposal — AI drafts executive summary, our understanding, approach, delivery model, sample profile, commercials and next steps. Print-ready.

Deal Risk Check

Every deal now shows a Risk badge (red/amber/green) on the Deals list based on activity gap + stage time. Open a deal → Risk Check → AI explains and suggests a recovery action.
