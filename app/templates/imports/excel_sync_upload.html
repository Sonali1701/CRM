How Excel Sync works

  1. Upload your team's Excel sheet (.xlsx) — any number of worksheets
  2. Choose which worksheets to include
  3. Map your columns to CRM fields (status, follow-up notes, follow-up date)
  4. Sync: existing contacts are updated, new ones are created

What gets synced

Contact fields (name, email, company…)
Status updates
Follow-up notes → Activity logged
Follow-up dates → Task reminder

Matching is done by email address. Contacts without an email match are created as new.
